HOW TO PLAY

  1. Build your Starship stack. Every choice trades mass, thrust, risk and margin.
  2. Crew the launch: a flight director, propulsion lead, weather officer and recovery lead. Each has a trait.
  3. Weather: read winds aloft, lightning, temperature and sea state. Call GO, SCRUB, or risk it.
  4. Ascent: ↓/S throttle down through max-Q, ↑/W back up. Press SPACE to stage when the landing reserve hits the green band.
  5. Catch: ↑/W and ↓/S set throttle (light the landing burn), ←→/AD steer. Drop between the chopsticks slower than the catch limit.
  6. Bad builds fail. An overweight stack, missing fins, or a thin heat shield will show up in flight, and the report tells you which part did it.

On a phone, use the on-screen buttons. M toggles sound.

Super Heavy can't hover: even at minimum throttle it barely holds itself up. Light the landing burn late but not too late.
